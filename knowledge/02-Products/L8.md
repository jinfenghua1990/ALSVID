## Source-attributed reference facts

The manufacturer describes the reference as a 20-inch carbon folding platform with a carbon fork. Its stated reference build includes:

- SHENGYI 250 W rear-hub motor, torque sensor, and up to 40 Nm maximum torque.
- LG 36 V / 10 Ah lithium-ion battery.
- Four-bar rear suspension with a DNM air shock. This is separate from the main-frame folding hinge.
- KMC chain and Shimano 7-speed derailleur as the reference drivetrain; belt drive is an optional project configuration.
- Shimano MT200 hydraulic disc brakes and Schwalbe 20 × 1.75 tires.

These are supplier reference facts, not a confirmed ALSVID production specification. Present them only as a **reference configuration** with that qualification.

The German source also describes a 350 W motor option and a 14.5 kg platform target. Do not show either on the consumer page until the intended configuration, market compliance, and configured sample are confirmed. Do not publish range or load claims from the reference page.
