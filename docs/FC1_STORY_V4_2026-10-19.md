## Changes
Six linked feature cards introduce the product narrative. A compact sticky chapter
navigation follows the metrics. Existing low-step, folding, suspension, drivetrain,
battery, configurator, gallery, specifications and FAQ remain available.
The power chapter uses the real drivetrain photograph; the cockpit stays in the
gallery. A seatpost-battery photograph and a braking/everyday-equipment chapter
retain the sample's geometry, rear rack, tan-wall tyres, rear hub and rear shock.
No synthetic intermediate folding position, battery extraction, colour rendering
or new product claim is created.

V4 applies graphite/slate-blue surfaces, larger headings, deliberate photo spacing
and mobile reflow. IntersectionObserver reveals headings; a passive, animation-frame
scheduled listener updates reading progress and gentle desktop hero travel.
Content is visible without JavaScript. Reduced motion is respected, including
preference changes while the page is open. Original interactive components are reused.
